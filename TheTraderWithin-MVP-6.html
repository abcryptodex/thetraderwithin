Frequently asked questions

How does the DEX calculator work?

Enter the market-cap entry, investment amount and your preferred reward-to-risk ratio. The calculator estimates a Stop Loss at the default 25% investment risk and a Take Profit target.

How does Futures planning work?

Enter the entry price, margin, leverage and Long or Short direction. Review the estimated Stop Loss and Take Profit before using the plan.

Are prices and balances live?

No account balance is connected. Market prices may come from a public data provider and can be delayed or unavailable.

Where is my trade journal saved?

In this MVP, plans are saved in local browser storage on this device. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Can I reset my password or enable 2FA?

Account login, password management and real two-factor authentication are not connected in this version.
